# Learning From Palantir POC

## Focus

The team has almost no other responsibilities. This allows them to work on one problem only and get into the weeds to understand.

They are extremely efficient and prepared because of this. All of their work revolves around us.

Maximize meeting time.

---

## Data

Distribution is very complex in terms of rebates, SPA, inventory, shipping - so many things.

Trying to get all the correct data is a multi-week endeavour with so many moving parts and people involved.

This work happens once for each area business problems, are there other problems in the same area that use similar data?

---

## Presentations

Their presentations are made early. They put a lot of effort into planning, structuring, timelines, and clear goals from both sides.


---

## Decisions

**No decisions are made on their own** - they ensure that stakeholders agree with every decision that is made.
