- The venture targets micro SMB (0–20 seats), customers we don't really serve today.

  These customers don't know they have a problem; they buy at Best Buy.
- What's needed: reach, scale, low cost to serve, and a level of digital service that doesn't exist today.

  2. Build a digital experience customers want (the "Agentic team for Digital" is close to this).

     rebuilt AI-native, then the vendor side.
